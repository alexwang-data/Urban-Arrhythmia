How to read the monitor

One district at a time. Its border becomes three moving lines on the left — a heartbeat — and three scores on the right. Step through with Before / Next.

The three pulses

  • Radial — how far the border sits from the center.
  • Curvature — how sharply the border turns.
  • Envelope — the overall shape, smoothed.

The three scores

  • Polsby–Popper — the main score, shown raw → shoreline-adjusted. The bar shows how much of the border is coast.
  • Reock — how well the shape fills a circle drawn around it.
  • Convex Hull — how much it reaches out in tendrils or notches.

Stable / Warning / Critical use the shoreline-adjusted score. A district is flagged for how it was drawn, not for the coastline it follows. A Stable district can still show a wild pulse. That wiggle is water, not design.
